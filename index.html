Geographic context

Natural Earth 1:110m coverage is approximate, and overlaps do not imply exclusive ownership. Subdivision coverage is available only for the United States in the supplied layer. Percentages refer to the entire block; latitude bands are geometric, not climate classifications.

Reading the temporal chart

Thin lines show seasonal anomalies across 1950–2014. Thick segments fit the shaded analysis period. The target contrast is the difference in slopes, not the vertical separation of the lines.

Methods and data notes

GFDL-ESM4 historical simulation, 1950–2014. Each map color is A − B for two fitted seasonal trends in the selected period; the lines show seasonal anomalies relative to the 1951–1980 baseline. Global context uses cosine-latitude area weighting.

Winter assigns December to the following year. Season names use Northern Hemisphere month conventions globally. Adjacent 30-year windows overlap heavily; slider-to-slider changes are not independent observations.

Natural Earth 1:110m geographic coverage is approximate. These modeled patterns describe change without establishing causes.
